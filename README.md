## Superstore Data Analysis

Exploratory Data Analysis and Statistical Analysis of a Superstore business dataset using Python.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, discounts, quantities, and relationships between different business variables.

The dataset was cleaned and prepared before performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical analysis, data visualization, and business hypothesis analysis.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Calculate descriptive statistics
- Analyze sales, profit, quantity, and discount
- Identify relationships between business variables
- Detect unusual values using visualizations
- Test business-related hypotheses
- Generate meaningful business insights

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### Descriptive Statistics
Calculated:

- Mean
- Median
- Standard Deviation
- Quartiles (Q1 & Q3)

### Data Visualization

Created:

- Histograms
- Box Plots
- Correlation Heatmap
- Scatter Plots
- Category-wise Analysis

### Correlation Analysis

Analyzed relationships between:

- Sales & Profit
- Discount & Profit
- Quantity & Profit
- Sales & Quantity
- Sales & Discount

## 💡 Key Insights

Some important observations from the analysis:

- Sales and Profit show a moderate positive relationship.
- Discount and Profit show a moderate negative relationship.
- Quantity and Profit have a weak positive relationship.
- Sales and Quantity have a weak positive relationship.
- Sales and Discount show very little linear relationship.

> Note: Correlation shows association between variables and does not necessarily imply causation.
